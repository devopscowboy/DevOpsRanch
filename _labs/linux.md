A lab is a task you do on your own RHEL virtual machine. Nothing here connects to your machine. You type the commands, then check your work, and open the solution only after you have tried.

Each lab matches one lesson. Read the lesson first if a word in the task is new. Rows marked coming soon do not have a lab page yet.

The lessons are on the [Linux lesson track](/lessons/linux/).
