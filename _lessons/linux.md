These lessons follow Red Hat's published RHCSA (EX200) objectives for Red Hat Enterprise Linux 10. Commands are the RHEL 9 and 10 forms. Where RHEL 7 or RHEL 8 differs, the lesson says so.

The filesystem comes first, before the exam list, because every later command uses a path. Firewalld is one lesson. The exam names that task under networking and again under security.

Each published lesson has its own lab. Rows marked coming soon are the rest of the objectives. They are not links yet.

The short command list for this track is the [Linux cheat sheets](/cheatsheets/linux/). The exercises are the [Linux labs](/labs/linux/).
